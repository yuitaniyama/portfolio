### Two dashboards, but no clear home

This redesign did not begin with a feature request. It started with friction I experienced while using the product myself.

At the time, Account Home was effectively the first page after login, but it was not available as an item in the side navigation. To return to it, I had to click the account name in the header.

When I moved from Account Home to another page and later clicked "Dashboard" in the side navigation, I was taken to a different page — the Sales Dashboard.
