The team knew that hubstep was expanding into SFA, but there was not yet a shared, concrete understanding of how that experience should work.

Discussions could remain abstract because different people were reasoning from different mental models. Rather than waiting for every requirement to be fully defined, I began turning incomplete ideas into tangible, high-fidelity screens early.

Before designing the screens, I mapped the early requirements into a system-level flow to understand how features, states, and screens connected. This became a working reference for identifying relationships and shaping the initial wireframes.
