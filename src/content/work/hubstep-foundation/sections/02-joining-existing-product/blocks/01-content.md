I joined the project in April 2025, when hubstep's initial development was already underway.

At that point, the basic screen designs, visual tone, and part of the Design System were already in place, and Engineering had already begun implementation. For the first roughly three months, I worked alongside a senior web designer, inheriting the existing design while improving UI/UX in parallel with development.

From July 2025 onward, I continued as the team's sole designer.
