### Issues that surfaced from real-world use

From summer 2025 onward, sales and internal users began raising a growing number of improvement requests based on how they actually used the product day to day.

Working from **roughly 500 pieces of feedback**, I organized the issues with the existing screens and kept revisiting the UI and flow, **presenting multiple design options so the PdM could decide**, and coordinating implementation approach with Engineering.

As a result, most of the core screens I'd inherited early on ended up receiving **continuous UI/UX improvement.**
