1. **I started thinking beyond a single screen, to the experience before and after it.** Rather than just building the screen a spec called for, I began looking ahead to the surrounding flow and the points where users would get stuck.
2. **"Good design" came to include implementation and operation, not just the screen.** Working closely with engineers, I developed a perspective that accounts for implementation load and system constraints, not just usability.
3. **What counts as a "good product" depends on your role.** PdM, sales, and Engineering each prioritize and need different things, I learned that firsthand.
4. **I should have talked with stakeholders directly, earlier.** Looking back, engaging directly with sales earlier would have sped up shared understanding of problems and getting to agreement.
