### Why hubstep was needed

Before hubstep existed, **customers recreated their recipient list from scratch for every mailing** and shared it internally, often by email. Internal staff would then organize and check that data before running the mailing through the existing DM-related systems.

At the same time, **the aging legacy system and the security risk of handling personal data were becoming real problems.** hubstep was developed to move this workflow onto a new foundation, and to become a service where **customers themselves could store and manage their own address data, and accumulate and reuse mailing and delivery-failure history.**
